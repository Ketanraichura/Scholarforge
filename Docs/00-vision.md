

## Mission
Build an AI-native research workspace that enables users to upload documents,
query them semantically, generate citation-grounded responses, and construct knowledge graphs.

## Success Metrics

- Upload success > 99%
- Chat response latency < 5s
- Citation accuracy > 90%
- RAG precision@5 > 0.8

## Non Goals
- Plagiarism bypass
- Fabricated citations
- Unverifiable claims
